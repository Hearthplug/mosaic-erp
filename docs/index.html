How a business owner starts.

The simple version runs on one computer and guides the owner through the business. A technical partner is only needed when moving to a hosted, multi-user production setup.

1 · OPEN

Run the local trial

Install Python 3.10+, run python3 install.py and python3 app.py, then open Mosaic in the browser. No account is required.

2 · ANSWER

Describe the business

Work through plain questions about products, stores, sales channels, stock, credit, returns, services, staff, and tax registration.

3 · PREVIEW

Watch the sample business change

See screens, daily numbers, business terms, staff access, workflows, and tax settings adjust to each answer before saving.

4 · REVIEW

Bring in the right people

Use the clear blueprint to review choices with staff, an accountant, or an implementer instead of asking them to discover the business again.

5 · CONTROL

Save, explain, and undo

Keep versions and an audit history, preview changes, and roll back configuration when a decision needs to be reversed.

6 · EXPORT

Take the plan with you

Download the complete blueprint as JSON. The current product helps design and control the setup; it is not yet a daily billing or accounting ledger.

Questions a business owner may ask.

Straight answers about what Mosaic can do today and where an implementer is still needed.

Can I try it without an IT team?

Yes. The local trial needs Python 3.10+ and two commands. A production setup should be handled by a capable implementer.

Will it fit my type of shop?

The blueprint changes for major retail types, stores, online channels, credit, returns, expiry, warranty, services, and staff patterns.

Can it import my products, customers, or invoices?

Not yet. Blueprint and workspace export exist, but business-data import does not. Migration must be planned separately.

Can stores and staff have different access?

The blueprint models multi-store work and workspaces support viewer, editor, and owner roles. It is not yet a complete store transaction ledger.

Can I use it for billing and accounting?

No. Mosaic is a blueprint and configuration service, not a billing engine, financial ledger, or accounting replacement.

Does it calculate or file taxes?

No. Its tax guides help configure and uncover missing facts. An accountant should confirm classifications and current rules.

What if the internet fails?

The local version runs on the same computer without internet after download. A hosted or multi-store setup depends on the chosen network and infrastructure.

Who owns the data?

In a self-hosted setup, the business controls its environment. Mosaic supports blueprint export, workspace export, backups, and confirmed deletion.

How do backups, upgrades, and support work?

Backup/restore tools and runbooks are included. There is no promised managed support service. The business or implementer owns operations.

Which version should I start with?

Use the simple local trial to evaluate it. A hosted production setup uses external PostgreSQL with Docker or Kubernetes and needs an implementer.

Should my accountant or implementer be involved?

Yes for deployment, migration, accounting integration, and tax decisions. Mosaic reduces discovery work but does not replace professional review.

How much migration work is required?

It depends on current systems and data quality. With no automated business-data import, mapping and migration must be scoped before a switch.

What does it cost?

The source is MIT licensed with no software licence fee. Hosting, implementation, migration, operations, and professional advice can still cost money.

What is complete today?

The interview, tailored blueprint, previews, exports, version history, roles, audit, rollback, tax configuration, and deployment packages work. A full sales, invoice, inventory, and accounting ledger does not.

Deployment choices and verified checks

The local trial uses SQLite. Production packages use an external PostgreSQL service through Docker Compose or Kubernetes/Helm. The current release passed 82 application/security tests, PostgreSQL 17 tenant-isolation integration, strict Helm checks, deployment validation, a live container readiness test, signed amd64/arm64 publication, and the configured high/critical vulnerability gate.
