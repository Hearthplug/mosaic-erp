# Security policy (draft)
Owner: [OWNER REVIEW]. Effective date: [OWNER REVIEW]. Review at least yearly and after a major incident.

Use least privilege and individual accounts; require MFA for administrators where available. Separate production from development, keep production secrets in managed secret storage, and rotate credentials after compromise or departure. Protect customer data in transit with HTTPS and database connections with verified TLS. Record and review vulnerability reports privately at [OWNER REVIEW: security contact]. Prioritize a credible exploitable vulnerability immediately, contain it, and retain evidence. Review dependency and image scan results on each PR; do not bypass failing security gates without a written risk decision. Deploy only reviewed commits and maintain rollback and restore plans. Report and investigate anomalies and customer access complaints. Quarterly, review administrator access, restore readiness and the vendor list. Retain a dated evidence record; do not claim independent attestation until completed.
