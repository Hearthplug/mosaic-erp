# Useful differentiators for an interview-native ERP

These are product principles and staged gates, not current production claims.

## 1. Business twin before go-live

Turn interview answers into a working sandbox with the owner's products, roles, stores and sample week. Let the owner try a purchase, late delivery, sale, return, cash shortage and stock count. Every simulation explains what Mosaic changed and why. Go-live requires the owner and specialists to accept the simulated consequences.

## 2. "Tell me how your day went"

The owner can describe ordinary events in their own words. Mosaic proposes structured actions, shows the records, stock and money effects, and asks for approval before posting. Ambiguous money, tax, recipient, product or date facts never post silently.

## 3. Exception-first setup

Instead of asking about 200 features, ask what goes wrong: missing cash, expired stock, unpaid customers, wrong discounts, late suppliers, branch shortages. Configure controls and alerts around those pains, then prove them in the business twin.

## 4. Role rehearsal

Show the owner what each worker will see and let them rehearse a cashier, receiver, manager and accountant day. Explain access in plain language. Flag when one person can create and approve the same sensitive action.

## 5. Consequence cards

Every inferred setting carries: "Because you said...", "Mosaic will...", "This means...", confidence, effective date and who must verify it. A change preview shows screens, workflow, permissions, stock, reports and accounting effects before activation.

## 6. Adaptive simplicity

Start each role on the task they do most. Hide unused modules without deleting capabilities or records. Suggest changes only after repeated evidence, never mutate policy from behavior alone. The owner reviews every adaptation.

## 7. Global rule passport

Each transaction stores which verified tax/rules version was used, the transaction facts that selected it and whether statutory output was allowed. Rule changes produce a re-verification queue and affected-transaction preview, not silent retroactive changes.

## 8. Trustable recovery rehearsal

A visible "practice a failure" mode backs up a sandbox, simulates interruption, restores it and confirms stock, cash, open orders and trial balance. Recovery becomes a product experience, not an operator promise.

## Acceptance rule

A differentiator ships only when it has persistent state, API and layperson UI/chat behavior, RBAC, audit, imports/exports where relevant, PostgreSQL isolation/concurrency, recovery behavior and automated end-to-end tests. Otherwise it stays in this roadmap.
